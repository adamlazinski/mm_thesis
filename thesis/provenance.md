## How to read a 'no'

A `no` is not automatically a defect — it means the result predates that correction and has to be checked against it, or scoped. Three cases arise in this thesis and all three are stated where they occur:

- **GLFT experiments 07-11, 27, 33, 34, 63, 64** predate the closed-form fix. Contribution 17's addendum reruns the decisive one (experiment 117) on the corrected formula and reports both; the conclusion strengthens.
- **Maker experiments before 2026-06-11** predate taker-on-arrival and overstate maker fills. The queue-priority verdict was re-established on the corrected engine in experiment 62, which is what Chapters 5 and 6 cite.
- **Experiments 103 and 111** were re-run on 2026-11-19 under the corrected terminal accounting, and both the old and new figures are reported in Chapter 8 §4.


Where a chapter cites a pre-correction result without a rerun, it says so in text. This table exists so that claim can be audited rather than taken on trust.
